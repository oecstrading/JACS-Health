## Slide 6: Voice-First Patient Intake - The 60-70% Time Savings Solution
**Headline**: Replacing Clipboard Fatigue with Conversational Ease

**The Traditional Problem**:
- 5-10 page clipboard forms take 30-45 minutes
- 70% contain errors or missing data
- Seniors with arthritis, vision issues, or cognitive decline struggle

**The HealthMate Solution**:
- **Conversational AI interface**: Digital nurse asks one question at a time
- **Voice input for all fields**: Speech-to-text with medical term verification
- **Intelligent pre-fill**: Auto-populate from existing HealthMate data (60-70% time savings)
- **Visual review & sign**: Large-font display with tap-to-edit and digital signature
- **Texas Medical Association standard**: 6 sections (Demographics, Emergency Contacts, Insurance, Medical History, Medications, Allergies, HIPAA Consent)

**B2B Clinic Value**:
- Clinics save 20-30 minutes per patient appointment
- Reduce intake errors from 70% to near-zero
- Professional PDF generation for EHR integration

## Visual Design Guidelines

- **Large fonts**: 24pt minimum for body text, 36pt+ for headlines
- **High contrast**: Dark text on light backgrounds, avoid gray-on-gray
- **Minimal text per slide**: 5 bullet points maximum, use visuals to carry the message
- **Warm, human imagery**: Real seniors (diverse ages, ethnicities) using technology confidently
- **Professional but accessible**: Not sterile corporate, not overly casual
- **AARP brand colors**: Consider incorporating AARP's red/white/blue if appropriate for partnership pitch
