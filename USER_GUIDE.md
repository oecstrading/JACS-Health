# HealthMate User Guide

## Welcome to Your AI Health Companion!

**HealthMate** is designed specifically for older adults to help you manage your health with ease. This guide will help you get started.

---

## Getting Started

### Opening the App
1. Tap the **HealthMate** icon on your phone
2. The app opens to your **Home** screen with today's health summary

### Navigation
The app has **4 main sections** at the bottom:
- **Home** 🏠 - Your daily health dashboard
- **Assistant** 🎤 - Talk to your AI health companion
- **Health** ❤️ - Track medications, vitals, and activity
- **Settings** ⚙️ - Customize your preferences

---

## Home Screen

### Today's Summary
See your health at a glance:
- ✅ Medications taken today
- ❤️ Vitals logged
- 🚶 Steps walked

### Quick Actions
Large buttons for common tasks:
- **Talk to AI Assistant** - Ask health questions
- **Log Medication** - Mark medications as taken
- **Record Vitals** - Log blood pressure, weight, etc.
- **View Health Insights** - See your health trends

### Emergency Button
The **red phone button** in the top-right corner is for emergencies. Tap it to contact your emergency contacts quickly.

---

## AI Assistant

### Talking to Your Assistant
1. Tap the **Assistant** tab at the bottom
2. Tap the large **"Tap to Talk"** button
3. Speak your question clearly
4. The assistant will respond with voice and text

### What You Can Ask
- "How is my blood pressure doing?"
- "What medications do I take today?"
- "What should I eat for dinner?"
- "How am I doing this week?"

### Quick Suggestions
If you're not sure what to ask, tap one of the **suggestion chips** below the conversation.

---

## Health Tracking

### Medications
1. Tap the **Health** tab
2. Make sure **Medications** is selected at the top
3. Tap the **checkbox** next to each medication when you take it
4. The checkbox turns green when checked ✅

**Adding Medications:**
- Tap the **"+ Add Medication"** button
- Enter the name, dosage, and time
- (Coming soon in future update)

### Vitals
1. Tap the **Health** tab
2. Tap **Vitals** at the top
3. See your recent vital signs:
   - Blood Pressure
   - Heart Rate
   - Weight
   - Blood Glucose

**Recording Vitals:**
- Tap **"+ Record Vitals"** to log new readings
- (Coming soon in future update)

### Activity
1. Tap the **Health** tab
2. Tap **Activity** at the top
3. See your steps for today and daily checklist
4. Check off activities as you complete them

---

## Settings

### Customizing Your Experience
1. Tap the **Settings** tab at the bottom
2. Adjust these preferences:

**Preferences:**
- **Voice Assistant** - Turn voice interaction on/off
- **Medication Reminders** - Get notifications for medications
- **Dark Mode** - Easier on eyes at night

**Text Size:**
- Choose from Small, Medium, Large, or Extra Large
- Larger text is easier to read

**Health Settings:**
- **Emergency Contacts** - Add people to call in emergencies
- **Health Conditions** - Tell the app about your health (diabetes, high blood pressure, etc.)

**Account & Privacy:**
- **Privacy Settings** - Control who sees your health data
- **Account Management** - Update your profile

## Tips for Using HealthMate

### 1. Make It a Daily Habit
- Open the app every morning
- Check off your medications as you take them
- Log your vitals at the same time each day

### 2. Use Voice When Possible
- The AI Assistant can answer most questions
- Just tap and talk - it's easier than typing
- The assistant speaks back to you

### 3. Keep Emergency Contacts Updated
- Add your family members and doctor
- Include their phone numbers
- Update if contacts change

### 4. Check Your Health Summary
- Look at "Today's Summary" on the Home screen
- See what you've completed
- Stay on track with your health goals

### 5. Adjust Text Size
- If text is too small, go to Settings
- Choose "Extra Large" for easier reading
- The app remembers your preference

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: How do I add a new medication?
**A:** Tap Health → Medications → "+ Add Medication" button. (Feature coming in next update)

### Q: Can my family see my health data?
**A:** Only if you give them permission. You control who sees your data in Privacy Settings.

### Q: What if I miss a medication?
**A:** The app will remind you with a notification. You can still check it off later.

### Q: How does the AI Assistant work?
**A:** It uses advanced AI to understand your questions and provide helpful health information based on your data.

### Q: Is my health data secure?
**A:** Yes! All your health data is encrypted and stored securely. You control who can access it.

### Q: Can I use the app without internet?
**A:** Most features work offline. The AI Assistant needs internet to answer questions.

## Getting Help

### Need Assistance?

- **Large Text** - Adjustable in Settings
- **High Contrast** - Easy to read colors
- **Voice Control** - Talk instead of typing
- **Haptic Feedback** - Feel when you tap buttons
