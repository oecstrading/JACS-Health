## Welcome to HealthMate!

HealthMate is designed to help you take control of your health with easy-to-use tools for tracking medications, monitoring vital signs, and staying connected with your healthcare team and family. This guide will walk you through everything you need to know to get the most out of your HealthMate app.

### Who This Guide Is For

This guide is written for two groups of people:

**Seniors (Primary Users):** If you are using HealthMate to manage your own health, this guide will show you how to track medications, record vital signs, talk to the AI health assistant, and celebrate your health achievements.

**Caregivers (Family Members):** If you are helping a loved one manage their health, this guide will show you how to monitor their health status, receive alerts, and provide support from anywhere.

### What You Will Learn

In this guide, you will learn how to perform common tasks such as setting up medication reminders, connecting health devices like blood pressure monitors, tracking your daily health metrics, and communicating with your care team. Each section includes step-by-step instructions with clear explanations designed for users of all technology skill levels.

## 1. Getting Started

### 1.1 Downloading the App

HealthMate is available on multiple platforms, allowing you to access your health information from your phone, tablet, or computer.

**For iPhone or iPad Users:**

1. Open the **App Store** on your device
2. Tap the **Search** icon at the bottom of the screen
3. Type **"HealthMate"** in the search box
4. Tap **Get** next to the HealthMate app icon
5. If prompted, enter your Apple ID password or use Face ID/Touch ID
6. Wait for the app to download and install
7. Tap **Open** to launch the app

**For Android Phone or Tablet Users:**

1. Open the **Google Play Store** on your device
2. Tap the **Search** bar at the top
3. Type **"HealthMate"** and tap the search icon
4. Tap **Install** next to the HealthMate app
5. Wait for the app to download and install
6. Tap **Open** to launch the app

**For Computer Users (Windows, Mac, or Chromebook):**

1. Open your web browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge, or Firefox)
2. Go to **healthmate.app** (or the web address provided to you)
3. The app will load in your browser
4. You can bookmark this page for easy access later
5. Some browsers will offer to "Install" the app - this creates a shortcut on your desktop

### 1.2 Creating Your Account

When you first open HealthMate, you will need to create an account to keep your health information secure and private.

**Step-by-Step Account Creation:**

1. On the welcome screen, tap **Create Account**
2. Enter your **email address** (this will be your username)
3. Create a **password** (must be at least 8 characters with a mix of letters and numbers)
4. Enter your **full name** as you would like it to appear in the app
5. Enter your **date of birth** (this helps personalize health recommendations)
6. Review the **Privacy Policy** and **Terms of Service** by tapping the links
7. If you agree, check the box that says **"I agree to the Terms of Service"**
8. Tap **Create Account**
9. Check your email for a verification message
10. Tap the link in the email to verify your account
11. Return to the app and log in with your email and password

**Important Privacy Note:** Your health information is private and secure. HealthMate uses bank-level encryption to protect your data. Your information is never shared with anyone without your explicit permission.

### 1.3 Setting Up Your Profile

After creating your account, you will be guided through setting up your health profile. This information helps HealthMate provide personalized recommendations and track your progress.

**Profile Setup Steps:**

1. **Basic Information:**
   - Confirm your name and date of birth
   - Select your preferred language
   - Choose your height and weight units (feet/inches or centimeters, pounds or kilograms)

2. **Health Conditions:**
   - Select any chronic conditions you have (diabetes, high blood pressure, heart disease, etc.)
   - You can skip this and add conditions later if you prefer

3. **Current Medications:**
   - Add medications you are currently taking
   - For each medication, enter the name, dosage, and how often you take it
   - You can also skip this and add medications later

4. **Emergency Contacts:**
   - Add at least one emergency contact (family member or friend)
   - Include their name, relationship, and phone number
   - This person will be notified if you use the emergency button

5. **Healthcare Providers:**
   - Add your primary care doctor's information
   - Include their name, specialty, phone number, and office address
   - You can add multiple doctors and specialists

6. **Notification Preferences:**
   - Choose whether you want to receive medication reminders
   - Select whether you want voice reminders in addition to notifications
   - Set quiet hours when you do not want to receive non-urgent notifications

After completing these steps, tap **Finish Setup** to access the main app.

## 2. Home Screen Overview

The Home Screen is your central hub in HealthMate. It shows a summary of your health status and provides quick access to all features.

### 2.1 Understanding the Home Screen

When you open HealthMate, you will see several sections on the Home Screen:

**Greeting and Emergency Button (Top):**  
At the top of the screen, you will see a personalized greeting such as "Hello! 👋" along with the question "How are you feeling today?" In the upper right corner is a red emergency button with a phone icon. Tapping this button will immediately call your designated emergency contact or 911, depending on your settings.

**Today's Summary Card:**  
This card shows your health activities for the current day. You might see information such as "2 medications taken," "Vitals logged this morning," and "3,245 steps today." This gives you a quick overview of your daily health progress without needing to open multiple screens.

**Quick Actions Section:**  
Below the summary card, you will find large, easy-to-tap buttons for common tasks. Each button has an icon and clear label such as "Talk to AI Assistant," "Log Medication," "Record Vitals," and more. These buttons provide one-tap access to the features you use most often.

**Navigation Bar (Bottom):**  
At the bottom of the screen, you will see five icons representing different sections of the app: Home, Health, Care Circle, Family, and Settings. Tapping any of these icons takes you to that section.

### 2.2 Navigating Between Screens

HealthMate is designed to be simple to navigate. There are two main ways to move between different parts of the app:

**Using Quick Action Buttons:**  
From the Home Screen, tap any of the Quick Action buttons to go directly to that feature. For example, tapping "Record Vitals" takes you to the screen where you can enter your blood pressure, weight, or other measurements.

**Using the Navigation Bar:**  
The navigation bar at the bottom of the screen is always visible, allowing you to switch between major sections at any time. Tap the Home icon to return to the Home Screen, tap the Health icon to see your health trends and history, tap the Care Circle icon to connect with family, tap the Family icon to see your caregiver dashboard, or tap the Settings icon to adjust your preferences.

**Going Back:**  
Most screens have a back arrow in the top left corner. Tapping this arrow returns you to the previous screen. On some devices, you can also swipe from the left edge of the screen to go back.

## 3. Managing Medications

HealthMate helps you remember to take your medications on time and tracks your adherence to ensure you are following your prescription schedule.

### 3.1 Adding a New Medication

To add a medication to your list, follow these steps:

1. From the Home Screen, tap **Log Medication**
2. On the Medications screen, tap the **+ Add Medication** button (usually in the top right corner)
3. Enter the **medication name** (for example, "Aspirin" or "Metformin")
4. Enter the **dosage** (for example, "81mg" or "500mg")
5. Select **how often** you take it:
   - **Daily:** Every day at the same time(s)
   - **Specific days:** Only on certain days of the week
   - **As needed:** When symptoms occur
6. If you selected Daily or Specific days, tap **Add Time** to set when you take it
7. Use the time picker to select the hour and minute
8. Tap **Done** or **Save** to confirm the time
9. If you take this medication multiple times per day, tap **Add Another Time**
10. Optionally, tap **Add Photo** to take a picture of the medication bottle (this helps you identify it later)
11. Tap **Save** to add the medication to your list

**Example:** Let's say you take Lisinopril 10mg every morning at 8:00 AM. You would enter "Lisinopril" as the name, "10mg" as the dosage, select "Daily," set the time to 8:00 AM, and tap Save.

### 3.2 Setting Up Medication Reminders

Once you have added a medication, HealthMate can automatically remind you when it is time to take it.

**Enabling Reminders:**

1. From the Medications screen, tap on the medication you want to set reminders for
2. Toggle the switch next to **Reminders** to turn it on (it will turn green or blue)
3. Toggle the switch next to **Voice Reminders** if you want to hear a spoken reminder in addition to the notification
4. Choose your **reminder sound** (gentle chime, bell, or custom)
5. Set **snooze options** (how long to delay the reminder if you are not ready to take it)
6. Tap **Save** to confirm your settings

**How Reminders Work:**  
When it is time to take your medication, you will receive a notification on your device. The notification will show the medication name and dosage, such as "💊 Time for Your Medication: Lisinopril - 10mg." If you have voice reminders enabled, you will also hear a spoken message saying "It's time to take your medication. Lisinopril, 10 milligrams. Please tap the notification to confirm."

### 3.3 Logging Medication Taken

When you take your medication, it is important to log it in the app so HealthMate can track your adherence.

**One-Tap Confirmation:**

1. When you receive a medication reminder notification, tap on the notification
2. The app will open to a confirmation screen
3. Tap the large **"I Took It"** button
4. The medication is now logged with the current date and time
5. You will see a checkmark and confirmation message

**Logging Manually (If You Missed the Notification):**

1. Open the HealthMate app
2. Go to the **Medications** screen
3. Find the medication you took
4. Tap the **Log** button next to it
5. Confirm the time you took it (the app will suggest the current time, but you can change it if needed)
6. Tap **Save**

**Snoozing a Reminder:**  
If you are not ready to take your medication when the reminder appears, you can snooze it:

1. Tap on the notification
2. Instead of tapping "I Took It," tap **Snooze**
3. Select how long to snooze: **5 minutes**, **10 minutes**, or **15 minutes**
4. The reminder will appear again after the selected time

### 3.4 Viewing Medication History

HealthMate keeps a complete record of when you have taken your medications, allowing you to see your adherence patterns over time.

**Viewing Your History:**

1. From the Medications screen, tap on a specific medication
2. Scroll down to the **History** section
3. You will see a list of all the times you logged taking this medication
4. Each entry shows the date, time, and whether it was taken on time, late, or missed

**Understanding Adherence Status:**

- **On Time (Green checkmark):** You took the medication within 30 minutes of the scheduled time
- **Late (Yellow clock icon):** You took the medication between 30 minutes and 2 hours after the scheduled time
- **Missed (Red X icon):** You did not log taking the medication, or you logged it more than 2 hours late

**Viewing Adherence Statistics:**

1. Tap the **Statistics** tab at the top of the medication detail screen
2. You will see your adherence rate as a percentage (for example, "95% adherence over the last 30 days")
3. Below that, you will see a breakdown:
   - **Doses taken on time:** Number and percentage
   - **Doses taken late:** Number and percentage
   - **Doses missed:** Number and percentage
4. A calendar view shows which days you took your medication (green) and which days you missed (red)

## 4. Tracking Vital Signs

Regularly tracking your vital signs helps you and your healthcare team monitor your health and detect potential problems early.

### 4.1 Recording Blood Pressure

Blood pressure is one of the most important vital signs to track, especially if you have hypertension or heart disease.

**Manual Entry:**

1. From the Home Screen, tap **Record Vitals**
2. Tap **Blood Pressure**
3. Enter your **systolic** reading (the top number) in the first box
4. Enter your **diastolic** reading (the bottom number) in the second box
5. The app will automatically record your **pulse rate** if your blood pressure monitor displays it (enter it in the third box if available)
6. Tap **Save**
7. The app will show you whether your reading is in the normal, elevated, or high range

**Example:** If your blood pressure monitor shows 128/82 with a pulse of 72, you would enter 128 in the systolic box, 82 in the diastolic box, and 72 in the pulse box.

**Understanding Blood Pressure Ranges:**

The app uses standard medical guidelines to categorize your blood pressure:

| Category | Systolic (Top Number) | Diastolic (Bottom Number) | Color Code |
|----------|----------------------|---------------------------|------------|
| Normal | Less than 120 | AND less than 80 | Green |
| Elevated | 120-129 | AND less than 80 | Yellow |
| High (Stage 1) | 130-139 | OR 80-89 | Orange |
| High (Stage 2) | 140 or higher | OR 90 or higher | Red |
| Crisis | Higher than 180 | OR higher than 120 | Dark Red |

If your reading falls into the Crisis category, the app will immediately alert you to seek emergency medical attention and will notify your designated caregivers.

### 4.2 Recording Weight

Tracking your weight helps monitor overall health, fluid retention, and the effectiveness of diet and exercise programs.

**Recording Weight:**

1. From the Record Vitals screen, tap **Weight**
2. Enter your weight using the number pad
3. The app will use your preferred unit (pounds or kilograms) based on your settings
4. Tap **Save**
5. The app will show you how your weight has changed since your last entry

**Viewing Weight Trends:**

1. After saving a weight entry, tap **View Trend**
2. You will see a graph showing your weight over time (last week, month, or year)
3. The graph uses a line to show whether your weight is increasing, decreasing, or staying stable
4. Below the graph, you will see your average weight for the selected time period

### 4.3 Recording Heart Rate

Your heart rate (pulse) indicates how fast your heart is beating and can reveal important information about your cardiovascular health.

**Recording Heart Rate:**

1. From the Record Vitals screen, tap **Heart Rate**
2. Enter your heart rate in beats per minute (bpm)
3. Optionally, select your activity level when you took the measurement:
   - **Resting:** Sitting or lying down for at least 5 minutes
   - **After exercise:** Within 10 minutes of physical activity
   - **During activity:** While walking or moving
4. Tap **Save**

**Understanding Heart Rate Ranges:**

| Category | Resting Heart Rate (bpm) | Status |
|----------|-------------------------|--------|
| Athlete-level | 40-60 | Excellent |
| Normal | 60-100 | Good |
| Elevated | 100-120 | Monitor |
| High | Above 120 | Consult doctor |

The app will alert you if your heart rate is unusually high or low for your age and activity level.

### 4.4 Recording Oxygen Saturation (SpO2)

Oxygen saturation measures how much oxygen your blood is carrying. This is especially important for people with lung or heart conditions.

**Recording SpO2:**

1. From the Record Vitals screen, tap **Oxygen Saturation**
2. Enter the percentage shown on your pulse oximeter (for example, 98%)
3. Tap **Save**

**Understanding SpO2 Levels:**

| SpO2 Level | Status | Action |
|------------|--------|--------|
| 95-100% | Normal | No action needed |
| 90-94% | Low | Monitor closely, contact doctor if symptoms |
| Below 90% | Dangerously low | Seek immediate medical attention |

If your SpO2 falls below 90%, the app will immediately alert you and your caregivers to seek emergency care.

### 4.5 Viewing Vital Signs History

HealthMate keeps a complete history of all your vital signs measurements, making it easy to spot trends and share information with your doctor.

**Accessing Your History:**

1. From the Home Screen, tap **View Health Insights**
2. Select the vital sign you want to view (Blood Pressure, Weight, Heart Rate, or Oxygen Saturation)
3. You will see a list of all your recent measurements, with the most recent at the top
4. Each entry shows the date, time, and value

**Viewing Trends and Charts:**

1. On the vital signs history screen, tap the **Chart** tab
2. Select a time range: **Week**, **Month**, **3 Months**, or **Year**
3. The chart will display your measurements over time as a line graph
4. Green areas indicate normal ranges, yellow indicates caution, and red indicates concerning levels
5. Tap any point on the chart to see the exact value and date

**Sharing with Your Doctor:**

1. From the vital signs history screen, tap the **Share** button (usually in the top right corner)
2. Select the time range you want to share (for example, "Last 3 months")
3. Choose how to share:
   - **Email:** Sends a PDF report to your doctor's email
   - **Print:** Prints the report if you have a connected printer
   - **Export:** Saves a PDF to your device for uploading to a patient portal
4. The report includes a summary table and charts of your vital signs

## 5. Connecting Omron Devices

HealthMate can automatically sync data from Omron blood pressure monitors and other health devices, eliminating the need to manually enter your readings.

### 5.1 Supported Omron Devices

HealthMate works with Omron devices that have Bluetooth or WiFi connectivity. Supported models include:

- **Omron Evolv (BP7000):** Wireless upper arm blood pressure monitor
- **Omron Complete (BP5450):** Wireless upper arm monitor with WiFi
- **Omron 10 Series (BP786N):** Bluetooth-enabled upper arm monitor
- **Omron 7 Series (BP761N):** Bluetooth-enabled upper arm monitor

If you are unsure whether your Omron device is compatible, look for the Bluetooth symbol on the device or check the user manual that came with it.

### 5.2 Pairing Your Omron Device via Bluetooth

Pairing your Omron device with HealthMate is a one-time process that takes just a few minutes.

**Before You Begin:**

- Make sure your Omron device has fresh batteries or is fully charged
- Keep your phone or tablet within 10 feet of the Omron device
- Make sure Bluetooth is turned on in your phone's settings

**Pairing Steps:**

1. From the Home Screen, tap **Omron Devices**
2. Tap the **+ Add Device** button
3. Select **Bluetooth** as the connection method
4. Tap **Start Scanning**
5. On your Omron device, press and hold the Bluetooth button (usually marked with a Bluetooth symbol) until the light starts blinking
6. Wait for your device to appear in the list on your phone (it may show as "Omron BP" followed by numbers)
7. Tap on your device name in the list
8. If prompted, confirm the pairing code shown on both your phone and the Omron device (the codes should match)
9. Tap **Pair** or **Confirm**
10. Wait for the connection to complete (you will see a success message)
11. Give your device a friendly name (for example, "Bedroom BP Monitor") so you can identify it easily
12. Tap **Save**

**Testing the Connection:**

1. Take a blood pressure reading with your Omron device as you normally would
2. After the reading is complete, the device will automatically send the data to HealthMate
3. Open the HealthMate app and go to **Record Vitals** > **Blood Pressure**
4. You should see your new reading at the top of the list with a Bluetooth icon indicating it was synced automatically

### 5.3 Connecting via WiFi (For WiFi-Enabled Devices)

Some Omron devices, like the Complete model, can connect directly to your home WiFi network and upload readings to HealthMate without needing your phone nearby.

**WiFi Setup Steps:**

1. From the Home Screen, tap **Omron Devices**
2. Tap the **+ Add Device** button
3. Select **WiFi** as the connection method
4. Follow the on-screen instructions to put your Omron device into WiFi setup mode (this usually involves pressing and holding a button on the device)
5. Your phone will temporarily connect to the device's WiFi network (it will have a name like "Omron-Setup")
6. Once connected, return to the HealthMate app
7. Enter your home WiFi network name (SSID)
8. Enter your WiFi password
9. Tap **Connect**
10. The Omron device will disconnect from your phone and connect to your home WiFi
11. Wait for the confirmation message (this may take 1-2 minutes)
12. Give your device a friendly name
13. Tap **Save**

**How WiFi Sync Works:**  
Once your Omron device is connected to WiFi, every time you take a reading, the device will automatically upload the data to the HealthMate cloud. The next time you open the HealthMate app, your new readings will appear automatically, even if your phone was not nearby when you took the measurement.

### 5.4 Managing Connected Devices

You can view and manage all your connected Omron devices from the Omron Devices screen.

**Viewing Device Status:**

1. From the Home Screen, tap **Omron Devices**
2. You will see a list of all your paired devices
3. Each device shows:
   - **Device name** (the friendly name you gave it)
   - **Connection status** (Connected, Disconnected, or Low Battery)
   - **Battery level** (if available)
   - **Last sync time** (when data was last received)

**Syncing Data Manually:**

If your device is connected but data has not synced automatically, you can trigger a manual sync:

1. From the Omron Devices screen, tap on the device you want to sync
2. Tap the **Sync Now** button
3. Make sure the device is turned on and within range
4. Wait for the sync to complete (you will see a progress indicator)
5. A success message will confirm how many new readings were synced

**Removing a Device:**

If you no longer use an Omron device or want to pair it with a different account:

1. From the Omron Devices screen, tap on the device you want to remove
2. Scroll to the bottom and tap **Remove Device**
3. Confirm by tapping **Yes, Remove**
4. The device will be unpaired from your account
5. Your historical data from this device will remain in HealthMate, but no new readings will be synced

## 6. Real-Time Vitals Monitoring

The Real-Time Vitals Monitor provides a live dashboard of your current health status, with instant alerts if any readings are abnormal.

### 6.1 Opening the Live Monitor

To access the real-time vitals monitoring dashboard:

1. From the Home Screen, tap **Live Vitals Monitor**
2. The screen will display large, easy-to-read panels showing your most recent vital signs
3. Each panel updates automatically when new data is received from connected devices

### 6.2 Understanding the Dashboard

The Live Vitals Monitor screen is divided into several sections, each showing a different vital sign:

**Heart Rate Panel:**  
This panel displays your current heart rate in large numbers (for example, "72 bpm"). Next to the number, you will see an animated heart icon that pulses at the same rate as your heart. The background color changes based on your heart rate: green for normal, yellow for elevated, and red for high.

**Blood Pressure Panel:**  
This panel shows your most recent blood pressure reading with both systolic and diastolic numbers (for example, "128/82 mmHg"). The panel also displays the time the reading was taken. The background color indicates whether your blood pressure is in the normal (green), elevated (yellow), or high (red) range.

**Oxygen Saturation Panel:**  
This panel displays your current SpO2 level as a percentage (for example, "98%"). A small lung icon animates to show breathing. The background is green for normal levels (95-100%), yellow for low levels (90-94%), and red for dangerously low levels (below 90%).

**Trend Indicators:**  
Each panel includes a small sparkline chart showing how that vital sign has changed over the past few hours. An upward-pointing arrow indicates the value is increasing, a downward arrow indicates it is decreasing, and a horizontal line indicates it is stable.

### 6.3 Setting Up Alerts

The Live Vitals Monitor can send you instant alerts if any of your vital signs go outside safe ranges.

**Configuring Alert Thresholds:**

1. From the Live Vitals Monitor screen, tap the **Settings** icon (gear symbol) in the top right corner
2. You will see customizable thresholds for each vital sign
3. For **Blood Pressure**, set your maximum systolic and diastolic values (your doctor may have given you specific targets)
4. For **Heart Rate**, set your minimum and maximum acceptable values
5. For **Oxygen Saturation**, set your minimum acceptable level
6. Toggle on **Alert Me** for each vital sign you want to monitor
7. Toggle on **Alert Caregivers** if you want your family to be notified when alerts occur
8. Tap **Save**

**Example:** If your doctor told you to keep your systolic blood pressure below 140, you would set the maximum systolic threshold to 140. If your blood pressure reading exceeds this value, you will receive an immediate alert.

**Receiving Alerts:**

When a vital sign exceeds your set thresholds, you will receive an alert in three ways:

1. **Push Notification:** A notification appears on your phone's lock screen and notification center
2. **In-App Alert:** A red banner appears at the top of the HealthMate app with the abnormal reading
3. **Caregiver Notification:** If you enabled caregiver alerts, your designated family members receive a notification with your reading and a suggestion to check on you

**Responding to Alerts:**

When you receive an abnormal vital sign alert:

1. Tap on the notification to open HealthMate
2. Review the reading and compare it to your recent history
3. If this is a one-time spike, you can tap **Dismiss** to acknowledge the alert
4. If the reading is consistently high or you feel unwell, tap **Contact Doctor** to call your healthcare provider
5. In an emergency (chest pain, difficulty breathing, severe symptoms), tap **Call 911**

### 6.4 Viewing Historical Trends

The Live Vitals Monitor also lets you see how your vital signs have changed over time.

**Accessing Trend View:**

1. From the Live Vitals Monitor screen, tap on any vital sign panel
2. The panel will expand to show a detailed chart
3. Use the time range selector to view **Last 24 Hours**, **Last Week**, or **Last Month**
4. The chart shows each measurement as a point, with a line connecting them to show the trend
5. Swipe left or right on the chart to scroll through time
6. Tap any point on the chart to see the exact value and timestamp

## 7. Using the AI Health Assistant

The AI Health Assistant is like having a knowledgeable health companion available 24/7 to answer questions, provide guidance, and help you understand your health information.

### 7.1 Starting a Conversation

To talk to the AI Health Assistant:

1. From the Home Screen, tap **Talk to AI Assistant**
2. You will see a chat interface similar to text messaging
3. Tap the microphone icon to speak your question, or tap the text box to type
4. Ask your question in plain language (for example, "What should I do if I forget to take my medication?")
5. Tap **Send** or release the microphone button
6. The AI will respond within a few seconds with helpful information

**Example Questions You Can Ask:**

- "What are the side effects of Lisinopril?"
- "Is my blood pressure reading of 135/88 normal?"
- "What foods should I avoid with diabetes?"
- "How can I lower my cholesterol naturally?"
- "What exercises are safe for seniors with arthritis?"
- "When should I call my doctor about a fever?"

### 7.2 Understanding AI Responses

The AI Health Assistant provides information based on trusted medical sources and your personal health profile. Here is how to interpret the responses:

**Personalized Advice:**  
The AI has access to your health profile, including your age, conditions, and medications. When you ask a question, the AI tailors the response to your specific situation. For example, if you ask about exercise and you have arthritis, the AI will suggest low-impact activities suitable for people with joint pain.

**Source Citations:**  
Many AI responses include references to medical sources. You will see small numbers in brackets like [1] or [2] in the response. These correspond to sources listed at the end of the message, allowing you to verify the information if desired.

**Disclaimers:**  
The AI will always remind you that its advice is for informational purposes only and is not a substitute for professional medical care. If your question involves symptoms that could be serious, the AI will recommend contacting your doctor or seeking emergency care.

**Follow-Up Questions:**  
The AI remembers the context of your conversation, so you can ask follow-up questions without repeating information. For example, after asking about Lisinopril side effects, you can simply ask "What should I do if I experience those side effects?" and the AI will understand you are still talking about Lisinopril.

### 7.3 Voice Conversations

If you prefer speaking to typing, you can have a voice conversation with the AI Health Assistant.

**Using Voice Mode:**

1. From the AI Assistant screen, tap the large microphone icon at the bottom
2. Speak your question clearly (you do not need to hold the button)
3. The AI will transcribe your speech and display it as text
4. After a moment, the AI will respond both in text and by speaking the answer aloud
5. When the AI finishes speaking, you can ask another question by tapping the microphone again

**Tips for Better Voice Recognition:**

- Speak at a normal pace (not too fast or too slow)
- Minimize background noise when possible
- Hold your phone 6-12 inches from your mouth
- If the AI misunderstands, you can tap the text to edit it before sending

### 7.4 Viewing Conversation History

HealthMate saves your conversations with the AI Health Assistant so you can refer back to previous advice.

**Accessing Past Conversations:**

1. From the AI Assistant screen, tap the **History** icon (clock symbol) in the top right corner
2. You will see a list of your recent conversations, with the most recent at the top
3. Each conversation shows the date and the first question you asked
4. Tap on any conversation to view the full exchange

## 8. Achieving Health Milestones

HealthMate celebrates your health achievements with animated badges and notifications, helping you stay motivated on your wellness journey.

### 8.1 Understanding Milestones

Milestones are goals that recognize your commitment to healthy habits. HealthMate tracks seven types of milestones:

**30-Day Medication Master:**  
Earn this milestone by taking all your medications on time for 30 consecutive days. This achievement shows excellent medication adherence and helps ensure your treatments are effective.

**10,000 Steps Champion:**  
Reach 10,000 steps in a single day to unlock this milestone. Walking 10,000 steps is associated with numerous health benefits, including improved cardiovascular health and weight management.

**7-Day Health Tracker:**  
Log your vital signs every day for 7 consecutive days to earn this badge. Regular monitoring helps you and your doctor spot trends and catch potential problems early.

**Blood Pressure Pro:**  
Maintain healthy blood pressure readings (below 140/90) for 30 consecutive days. This milestone demonstrates excellent blood pressure control, which reduces your risk of heart disease and stroke.

**7-Day Exercise Streak:**  
Exercise every day for 7 consecutive days to unlock this achievement. Regular physical activity is one of the most important things you can do for your health.

**Perfect Week:**  
Complete all your health goals (medications, vitals logging, exercise) for 7 consecutive days. This is the ultimate achievement, showing comprehensive commitment to your health.

**Health Score Elite:**  
Achieve a health score of 90 or higher. Your health score is calculated based on medication adherence, regular vitals logging, exercise, and maintaining healthy vital signs.

### 8.2 Tracking Your Progress

You can see how close you are to earning each milestone at any time.

**Viewing Milestone Progress:**

1. From the Home Screen, tap **Milestones & Badges**
2. You will see a list of all available milestones
3. Milestones you have not yet achieved show a progress bar indicating how close you are
4. For example, if you have taken your medications on time for 18 out of 30 days, the progress bar will be 60% full
5. Below each milestone, you will see specific progress details (for example, "18/30 days completed")

**Encouragement Messages:**  
When you are close to achieving a milestone (75% or more), HealthMate displays an encouragement message such as "🔥 Almost there! Keep going!" to motivate you to complete the goal.

### 8.3 Celebrating Achievements

When you reach a milestone, HealthMate celebrates your achievement with a special animation and notification.

**What Happens When You Unlock a Milestone:**

1. You receive a push notification with the message "🎉 Milestone Achieved! Congratulations! You've earned [Milestone Name] [Badge]"
2. When you open HealthMate, a celebration screen appears with confetti animation
3. Your earned badge is displayed prominently (for example, 🏆 for 30-Day Medication Master)
4. A message congratulates you and explains what you accomplished
5. Your family members (if you have added them to your Care Circle) receive a notification celebrating your achievement
6. The milestone is added to your achievement collection

**Viewing Your Badge Collection:**

1. From the Milestones screen, tap the **Completed** tab
2. You will see all the milestones you have achieved, displayed as colorful badges
3. Each badge shows the date you earned it
4. Tap on any badge to see details about that achievement

### 8.4 Sharing Achievements with Family

Your health achievements are automatically shared with family members in your Care Circle, allowing them to celebrate your progress and offer encouragement.

**How Family Notifications Work:**  
When you unlock a milestone, HealthMate sends a notification to all family members you have added as caregivers. The notification says something like "Great news! [Your Name] just achieved the 30-Day Medication Master milestone! 🏆" Family members can tap the notification to see details and send you a congratulatory message.

**Care Circle Feed:**  
Your milestones also appear in the Care Circle feed, which is a shared timeline visible to you and your caregivers. This feed shows your health achievements, making it easy for family to stay connected and celebrate your successes.
